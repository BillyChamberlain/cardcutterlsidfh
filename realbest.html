  Integrity: read text can come from separated sentences across any number of paragraphs. All intervening source text remains in smaller type, in original order. No generated evidence is inserted. Review qualifications, negations, citation and author intent before use. Your tournament or team may restrict AI assistance.
  
Workflow reference: NSDA: How to Cut Cards. The green/italic font treatment is your requested style, not a universal PF rule.
  
Privacy: local AI runs entirely in your browser. Jina Reader mode sends the article URL to r.jina.ai. Puter mode sends the article and your claim to the model provider through your own free Puter account. Groq/Gemini modes send them to that provider using your key. API keys are never stored, saved, or exported — they stay in this tab.
